Stacy Montemayor

Screenwriter



Stacy is an award-winning screenwriter with a background in journalism, technical writing, and historical research. Her screenplays are deeply grounded in nature and tell female-driven stories about people fighting for new, better lives for themselves and their communities. In 2025, she was a semi-finalist for The Writer’s Lab International, a program for women and non-binary screenwriters over 40 that is supported by Meryl Streep and Nicole Kidman. She was also honored with their Bold Voice Award, which recognizes exceptional originality and courage in storytelling.


Stacy volunteers as the Author Readings Coordinator for the Montana Writers Rodeo conference, and she’s currently co-developing a five-week script-to-screen filmmaking initiative to take place in the winter of 2027. When she’s not writing screenplays or volunteering, she researches and gives presentations on Montana’s Black history.


Originally from California, Stacy holds a degree in anthropology and has lived all over the American West, including the Bay Area, San Diego, Seattle, and Hawaii. In 2018, she moved to Mexico to spend some time connecting with her cultural roots, and after a couple of years living in Mexico’s bustling cities, she moved to Montana, where she built a very small house on a quarter acre and has a front yard full of wildflowers. In her free time, Stacy clears her head by lifting heavy weights and going on very long hikes on trails that start right outside her front door.


Featured Projects


  • CORPUS
    Feature
    Drama with elements of horror
    In an isolated community of zealots led by an all-powerful showman, a devout follower discovers the leader’s horrific abuses and embarks on a reckless mission of vengeance at any cost.


  • THE BLUE CASTLE
    Feature
    Drama
    Set in the 1920s, a subservient woman who's been stifled by her strict mother, receives a medical diagnosis that upends her world. She must decide if she's brave enough to stand on her own and reach for her own happiness. Based on the L.M. Montgomery novel.


  • THE SEA CHANGE
    Feature
    Drama/Near-Future Dystopia
    In a future where the climate has collapsed and society has violently unraveled, an order-obsessed and reclusive loner is forced from her home and finds herself responsible for a teen she barely knows. Together, they embark on a perilous journey across a fractured America in search of refuge, family, and the fragile hope of a new beginning.


  • FATHOMS
    Short Play
    Drama
    As catastrophic flood waters rapidly approach, a woman must convince her father to evacuate his home and abandon everything he holds dear.


  • ANKLE DEEP
    Novel
    An isolated loner hits rock bottom and reaches out to her estranged aunt, embarking on a new life in rural Montana.


  • LAUREL & KOA
    Novel
    In order to save a nearly extinct plant, a conservation botanist in Hawaii must gain the trust of a ranching family’s patriarch and son so they will allow her access to their land. She must shed her hardened exterior as she struggles to be open with the family.
